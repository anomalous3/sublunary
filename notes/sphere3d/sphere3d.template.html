The ecliptic, the Sun's path, is the warm ring; the Moon rides her own silver orbit, tilted 5°, crossing it at ☊ Rahu, the head, and ☋ Ketu, the tail. Each planet wears Lilly's orbs as caps of light, at itself and at every aspect point. When the Moon enters one from the applying side it lights, and the bulb takes the planet's color. Drag to turn the sky. With Sound on, the Moon is a low drone and each lit orb sounds its aspect above her, in Kepler's consonances: sextile a minor third, square a fourth, trine a fifth, opposition an octave. Underneath, the planets' own aspects: a chord joins two planets while they hold one, and sounds its interval above the slower planet, softly; squares and oppositions beat. With voices by sign, each sounds in its sign: the element sets the tone (fire bright, earth hollow, air a flute, water nearly pure), the modality how it moves (cardinal pulses, fixed swells, mutable wavers).
