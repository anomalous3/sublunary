    Coming up: what the sky does to each of them

      Fold the zodiac. Height is a planet's place in the circle. Folding stacks points that are the same angle apart, so planets in that aspect land at the same height, and where two lines cross the aspect is exact. Each button shows the shape it brings together.

      Each row is a point in the birth chart. Each shape is an aspect swelling to exactness. Striped means a square or opposition. Drag to move through time, or scroll on the wheel. Arrow keys: a day; with Shift: a week.
