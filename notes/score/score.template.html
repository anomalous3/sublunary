Sublunary: Cantata

Begin with a birth chart

Cantata reads a birth chart, then the sky moving across it: this week, the Moon's laps, the years ahead, and what passes between two people. It shows what's in play and when, never a verdict on what will happen.

Everything is computed on this device. Nothing you type is sent anywhere, and your chart stays in this browser.

Or look around first, with two invented people or Kepler and Lilly.

Layers and settings

What's in play, and when. The wheel is the birth chart with the sky moving around it. Below, each row is one aspect: its thread rises toward the line as the aspect comes exact, then falls away. Rows that begin, end or peak in these days come first; the slow ones that last all season wait underneath.

How to read the wheel

Inside is your birth chart. Around it, each planet leaves a trail across the days shown below: faint where the window begins, brightest at the playhead, dimmer ahead. A trail that steps inward and doubles back is a retrograde.

Each aspect in focus gets a faint arc on the outer edge, as wide as its orb, at the point where it's exact. Where a trail runs through one, that stretch lights up in your planet's colour. It's the same stretch of days as that aspect's thread below rising to its line. Lines across the middle join the planets in orb right now to the points they touch. Tap a lit stretch to read it.

The birth chart. The sky at the moment and place of birth: the twelve signs around the edge, the twelve houses inside them, and lines for the angles between planets. Headline gives the few things that stand out most. Full reads every planet, with the older nuances: dignity, the Sun's glare, which planets host each other. Everything adds the long tail.

The Moon, lap by lap. The zodiac runs across and time runs down, so each trip around the circle is one long stroke, about a month. Vertical lines are your natal points: she passes each at the same place every lap, so her contacts stack into columns. Curves are the planets drifting. She's brightest near full. While she's void of course the line goes out, and she travels a pale hatched channel.

Two charts, and what passes between them

Add a second person's birth details. Two lays both charts on one wheel and reads the aspects between them, then shows what the sky does to each of you over the coming weeks, and where a single planet touches you both at once.

If they've sent you a Cantata link, open it and choose "Add it beside mine". Like yours, this chart stays in this browser.

The list reads the aspects between the two charts, strongest first, and the lens changes which lead. Below it, time runs across: the first chart's transits rise above the spine and the second's hang below, with a bright bridge wherever one planet touches both at once.
